In 2026, I hiked the John Muir Trail with my partner: ~250 miles and ~50,000' gain through California's Sierra Nevada mountain range, over 22 days. We hiked southbound, starting on the 15th of June from Happy Isles trailhead in Yosemite National Park, and ending on July 6th on the summit of Mt. Whitney on the border of Sequoia National Park and Inyo National Forest.

## Photos

I shot these on Ilford HP5 with a Nikon F3 and a 50mm lens. They cover about two days of the trail, from Goodale Pass to Evolution Basin.
