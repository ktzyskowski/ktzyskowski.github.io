Kevin Zyskowski

Applied ML Researcher

Hi! I’m Kevin (he/him/his), an applied ML researcher based in Seattle, WA. I earned my B.S. and M.S. from Northeastern University in Boston, where my master’s work was advised by Prof. Rajagopal Venkat.

I’m currently doing independent research on biometric identification of players in third-person shooter games, with the goal of catching ban evasion. Previously, at Staples, I built an evaluation framework for information retrieval systems, and fine-tuned transformer embedding models for product matching.

I’m looking for applied ML roles. If you’re working on something interesting, feel free to reach out at ktzyskowski@gmail.com. Email is the best way to reach me! You can also find me on GitHub and LinkedIn.

Outside work and research, you can find me climbing outdoors or practicing guitar, usually under the supervision of my cat, Pasha.
